## rc#1 MED: the unlinked read's LIMIT 11 (:426) is its own window, not the candidate cap
Plan: (B) Step 3, "Every LIMIT 10/11 becomes `LIMIT $n`, bound to cap or cap+1"; (E) leaves :426 unverified.
Evidence: parcel-lookup.ts:426 `ORDER BY address, ap.address_point_id LIMIT 11`. The rows are used only for `unlinked.length > 0` and `new Set(unlinked.map((u) => u.address)).size === 1` (the pin rule). There is no slice, no overflow flag and no candidate list. Other sites are the cap: :317/:345/:365 (LIMIT 11 probe), :388/:393. :456 and :472 list candidates. If :426 follows the cap, then at cap 2 (the plan's min) it reads 3 rows, so a street with more unlinked points all printing one address could show a pin from a cut window. If it stays typed, the plan's regex `LIMIT 1[01]\b` still matches it, so the new test stays red.
Fix: state in Step 3 that :426 is a separate window. Either bind it to cap+1 deliberately and raise the cap min to 10 (min 2 breaks the pin rule), or keep it as `LIMIT 11` and exclude that line from the regex. A new setting for it is not named by a ruling (RR-0392).
