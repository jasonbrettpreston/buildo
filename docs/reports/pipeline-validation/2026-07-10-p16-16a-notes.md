## PLB 45% evidence-recall gap — root cause [Gemini HIGH]
Verdict: **primarily a ground-truth / harness-mapping artifact, NOT a production classifier bug.**
The inference layer deliberately never touches narrow-scope permits (a `narrow-permit-gains-no-inference`
regression lock ships in 16C), so this gap is an **evidence-layer floor**, not closable by P16.

Evidence — inspection stages on `Plumbing(PS)` permits in the corpus (distinct permits):

| stage | maps to (STAGE_TRADE) | permits |
|---|---|--:|
| Plumbing Final | plumbing | 29 |
| Water Distribution | plumbing | 28 |
| Drain/Waste/Vents | drain-plumbing, plumbing | 27 |
| Occupancy | [] (non-trade) | 26 |
| Sewers/Drains/Sewage System | drain-plumbing | 10 |
| Fire Service | fire-protection, plumbing | 9 |
| Water Service | plumbing | 7 |

A Plumbing(PS) permit's ground-truth trade set spans **{plumbing, drain-plumbing, fire-protection}**,
but `NARROW_SCOPE_CODES.PLB/PSA = ['plumbing']` attaches only `plumbing` → recall ≈ 1/3 per permit.
The split is a **taxonomy artifact**: the one plumber's DWV rough-in (Drain/Waste/Vents, Sewers) is the
separate `drain-plumbing` trade, and the `Fire Service` inspection on a plumbing permit is the plumbing
standpipe/fire-line, not the `fire-protection` trade. Broadening `NARROW_SCOPE_CODES.PLB` to include
`drain-plumbing`/`fire-protection` would raise the metric but degrade precision on 55,876 live
`Plumbing(PS)` permits (attaching a distinct drain-plumbing trade to every plumbing permit is not
correct), and is a far larger blast radius than 16A's cheap-correctness scope — **NOT taken**.

Disposition: documented as a known evidence-layer floor; the 16B harness may optionally collapse
`Drain/Waste/Vents`/`Fire Service` to plumbing-only in PLB-context for a fairer recall denominator —
recorded as an obligation in the 16B eval report, not a P16 gate.
