## 1. The policy in plain words (ten lines)
1. When anyone opens a lot from the search page, the report numbers for that lot are worked out once, behind the 35 second loading screen. That covers the main page and See details together. **[SUPERSEDED 2026-11-20 by RR-0176, ~/Buildo-scratch/queues-20261113/MORNING-DECISIONS.md:785: no 35 s cap; the animation keeps its own length and the button shows when the report and See details are loaded; prior text kept]**
2. The same happens when a user tracks a lot, or types in their own lot size, house or renovation inputs.
3. The result is saved with its stamp (Layer 2 table_version, evaluator_version, settings sha), for that user and for everyone else who opens the lot later.
4. When a saved result's stamp is not the current stamp, it is never shown. It is worked out again.
5. Nothing is saved for a lot nobody opened or tracked. There is no all-lot fill (the 54 to 66 day job is gone).
6. The saved result may be deleted at any time without harm, because a missing result is just worked out again. (Purging is a proposal, open point 1.) **[SUPERSEDED 2026-11-20 in part by "1. yes" (~/Buildo-scratch/queues-20261113/MORNING-DECISIONS.md:719) and PRG-1 a (~/Buildo-scratch/queues-20261113/MORNING-DECISIONS.md:738): the purge of untracked, un-owned records after 90 days is ruled (Spec 69a note at 69a:696); OPEN: are owner-input records purged; prior text kept]**
7. The big per-field tables (the 309 long form) stay only for the 25 test lots, the 1,921 cohort and the proof set.
8. Proof and testing use a sample of lots, never an all-lot table.
9. Cost for the opened lot is the cost rate x escalation x adjustment x the lot's saved size, worked out each time the report opens (it is not saved, so a rate change never leaves a stale price).
10. The first open can still take up to 33.6 s as built (median 10.9 s, MEASURED 20 lots), so task 5.11 (speed up the garden-suite split) stays and the screen setting rises to 35 s. The 1 s budget applies to reading a saved result. **[SUPERSEDED 2026-11-20 by RR-0176 (~/Buildo-scratch/queues-20261113/MORNING-DECISIONS.md:785): the setting does not rise to 35 s; the 1 s scope sentence is policy text, OPEN; prior text kept]**

+export async function runLookupFlow(input: { q?: string | undefined; parcelId?: string | undefined; semi?: string | undefined }): Promise<LookupFlowResult> {
