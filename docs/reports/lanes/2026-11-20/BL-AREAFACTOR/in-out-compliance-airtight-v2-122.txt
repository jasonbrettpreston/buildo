#### 6.6.1 Registry and ledger truth — generated or witnessed, declared == observed (Spec 124 §5 R-BF, operator ruling 2026-09-30)

**(a) Requirement.** **Name: McDonald's Airtight V2 (registry & cross-step).** V1 (Spec 124 R-BA, 2026-09-26) makes a step's FORM airtight — five words, each a closed-answer gate; it never checks that what a descriptor declares is what the step actually touches. V2 adds TRUTH: the step's own registry and the cross-step ledger are generated or witnessed, declared == observed. A step is V2-built when its captures run under the Phase 1 witness. For every sources-chain step (then every chain), the four-part step registry — FILES (the §4.1 set + owner spec), DATA (every table AND column read or written, each with its migration), CONSUMERS (every reader of the step's tables/columns and `records_meta` keys, across all chains and `src/`), TESTS — and the §6 cross-step ledger (each step's reads/writes and producer steps) are **100% mechanical**: generated from code/runtime or witnessed from executed SQL, never hand-authored; and **100% accurate**: declared == observed at column level, verified by a gate with closed answers, zero allowlist, strict zero (R-BC). This hardens LDG-10's table-edge class above: the "runtime lineage" it reconciles against must be observed, never the declaration re-emitted. R-BE already generates the Target Files blocks mechanically, but from the declarations — generated is not accurate until its sources are witnessed.

**(b) Mechanism (designed, not built).** An executed-SQL witness during golden captures — every statement parsed with the real Postgres grammar (`libpg-query`), touched ≡ declared; a fixture-time guard; observed `pipeline_meta` published alongside declared; manifest `chain_args` generated from the descriptor; `inputs.reads.steps` derived. Queued plans: `.cursor/wf2_registry_truth_active_task.md` (three phases: WITNESS · DERIVE · WIRE OR DELETE) and `.cursor/wf3_link_parcels_full_mode_active_task.md` (the `chain_args` generator, Phase 2's prerequisite). The Phase 1 WITNESS gate (#44) must carry the exact string `R-BF WITNESS GATE (#44)` in `scripts/analysis/step-validate.mjs`; the R-BF lock in `src/tests/spec124-register-rulings.logic.test.ts` holds (c) at NOT MET while that marker is absent and requires MET once it is present — both directions RED.
