- **[HIGH]** (step 4): Empty-entry footgun. `Files:` with trailing whitespace matches `/^Files:\s*(.+)$/m` by backtracking (`\s*` yields one space to `(.+)`), and `Files: a, ,b` or a trailing comma produces `""` entries after split/trim. If entry matching is implemented as `p.startsWith(entry)` rather than `p === entry || p.startsWith(entry + '/')`, the empty entry grants **every file in the repo**. The test list only covers "empty `Files:` value", not empty *array elements*. Fix: reject empty entries explicitly (`if (!entry) continue` — or better, error), and state the boundary predicate verbatim in the plan.
