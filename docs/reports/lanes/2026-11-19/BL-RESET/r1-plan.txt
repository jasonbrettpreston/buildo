- **[HIGH]** (Description → "In plain words", ≈line 23; contradicted by AC #1 and the "Wrong day" bullet): The narrative states the **inverted** direction of the fix. It says a customer whose reset is 9 pm on 8 December "sees *until 8 Dec 2026* instead of *until 9 Dec 2026*", i.e. present-tense value = 8 Dec, desired = 9 Dec. Independent check: 9 pm EST 8 Dec = `2026-12-09T02:00Z`; the UTC calendar day is **9 Dec** (that is what ships today), the Toronto day is **8 Dec** (that is the fix). AC #1 says `2026-12-09T02:00:00Z` must serve **8 Dec 2026**, and the "Wrong day" bullet says "the UTC date is one **later** than the Toronto date". So the narrative teaches the opposite of the AC. Also "one day **late**" (same paragraph) reads as "the shown date is behind", when the shown date is **ahead**. Why it's a problem: "In plain words" is the paragraph a human implementer reads first and remembers; a lane that codes the sign from it inverts the whole task and every new test. How to fix: rewrite unambiguously — *"today the line reads 'until 9 Dec 2026' (the UTC day); after this task it reads 'until 8 Dec 2026' (the Toronto day of the same instant)"* — and drop "late" for "one day ahead".

- **[HIGH]** (Plan steps → "Device side"; AC #6 "additive_only"): **No deploy-ordering story for new client + old server.** `resetsOn` stays on the wire (good), but the plan makes `reportsLeftShown` *require* a non-empty `resetsOnDisplay` and drops the instant from `EntitlementLine`, so a new mobile build talking to an un-deployed (or rolled-back) server gets `undefined` for the label and **silently hides the reports-left line**. There is no minimum-server gate, no fallback to the (still-served) `resetsOn`, and no rollback note. Mobile binaries lag servers by weeks and cannot be rolled back. Why it's a problem: the exact user-visible element this task exists to fix disappears, and AC #3/#5 (goldens, TZ tests) cannot catch it because they mock the new shape. How to fix: either (a) keep a defensive fallback in `reportsLeftShown` when `resetsOnDisplay` is absent but `resetsOn` is present (log a warning, don't render), or (b) state an explicit release gate "mobile build N ships only after server commit X is live in prod" in the plan and in the lane REPORT, and name the rollback (server stays additive, so a client rollback is safe; a server rollback is not).

- **[MEDIUM]** (Plan steps → "Formatter approach"; AC #1's "toronto day"): **Unstated seasonal wobble the fix introduces.** The context establishes that the reset instant is stepped "by whole months **in UTC**" and "keeps the anchor's UTC time of day". A Toronto calendar day is UTC−4 (EDT) or UTC−5 (EST). For anchors whose UTC time-of-day is in **[04:00, 05:00)**, the Toronto local time is 23:00–23:59 in winter (previous local date) but 00:00–00:59 in summer (same local date): the served label will **alternate by one day between winter and summer cycles** for those accounts. Today's UTC label is constant, so this is a *new* month-to-month inconsistency the plan never names, AC #1 never covers, and the twelve-month test (mid-month instants) cannot see. Why it's a problem: a customer will report "my reset date changed from the 8th to the 9th" and the lane will have no answer. How to fix: state the behavior, pin it with two tests (a `04:30Z` anchor in January → previous day, the same time-of-day in July → same day), and note it as accepted-by-design in the REPORT. Do not "fix" it with arithmetic.

**Verdict:** the mechanism is right — server-formatted Toronto label, numeric month parts plus a fixed twelve-name table, additive contract, no client arithmetic — and the three AC #1 instants check out arithmetically (`02:00Z`→8 Dec, `05:00Z`→9 Dec, `03:30Z`→9 Jul). But the plan is not implementation-ready. Three things must be settled before code: the plain-words paragraph **inverts the fix direction** and must be rewritten so it cannot be read against AC #1; there is **no compatibility story for a mobile build reaching an older server** (the line silently disappears, and no rollback is named); and the field name is being self-authorised against a task that says it must be claimed or ruled. Behind those sit four design problems that will bite in review or in production: a second month formatter that leaves "Sept" on adjacent pages (against the context's own "owed in this task"), a newly introduced winter/summer day flip for anchors in `[04:00, 05:00)` UTC that no test covers, a TZ-invariance AC that the proposed test mechanism cannot actually prove, and a "425 unchanged" claim asserted rather than measured alongside unlisted generated outputs. Fix the three blockers, fold the four mediums into the plan, and this is a small, well-grounded task; ship it as written and the first bug report is "my reset date jumps between the 8th and the 9th" or "the reports-left line disappeared after the update".
