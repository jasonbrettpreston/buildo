## Problems

`file:line` is given where a line number was resolvable in this pass; otherwise the section is named.

**P-1 — M-205: a permissive reduction is used as a bound (highest impact).** 10.10.40.70(4), as the row quotes it: *"may be reduced to 0.45 metres if there are no windows or doors in that side of the building"*. This is a permission, not a requirement: 0.9 m is lawful. Spec 78 P5.2 item 1 (line 757, *Sides*) and P5.10 `main_side_setbacks_m` make 0.45 m the headline in R and put the lawful 0.9 m inside `blank_side_wall` ("with them the side yard is {X} m"). For a **maximum** build this overstates the R side envelope by 0.9 m per side, on every R parcel (104,546 by M-200's count), and also opens the M-200 storey with the free share at 0.45 m. The operator rule ("default the smallest setback — rest disclosed") was written for design setbacks (LC-35, suite openings); applying it to a boundary-to-boundary envelope turns a choice into an assumption. Either the headline is 0.9 m with 0.45 m disclosed, or the row must say why a permission is taken as a bound.

**P-2 — M-205: 10.10.40.70(4)'s chapeau is never quoted.** UNVERIFIED whether the clause says *"despite (3)"*, is per side or per building, or reaches a flat-roof two-storey. M-205, P5.2 and P5.10 rest the whole R side reading on "(4)" with no text.

**P-3 — M-200 (systemic): "RATIFIED" rows with no observable.** The admission says "OBSERVABLE: Spec 78 P5.13 R flat-roof vector"; the status cell says the vector is **OWED**. The row is ratified on a reading, not on any behaviour that exists. Every row here except M-200 and M-206 says "P5.13 (**OWED, ①**)". The register should distinguish "reading settled" from "observable"; today both read RATIFIED.

**P-4 — M-199: one concept, three names.** The stricter reading is `secondary_suite_share_reading` (P5.12, Spec 78:1297) — the operator ruling quoted in the brief calls it `suite_interior_reading`, and grepping the repo for `suite_interior_reading` returns 0 hits. The row calls it "the stricter main-unit-only reading". Code, ruling and prose use three spellings of one idea.

**P-5 — M-199: the denominator, and the basement pass.** 150.10.40.40(1) verbatim is *"the interior floor area of the dwelling unit within which it is located"*. On RI (houseplex conversion, P5.6 `renovation_interior`) the "whole house" and "the dwelling unit" diverge and the chosen reading **overstates** the lawful suite; M-199 calls the alternative "stricter", which is the wrong word for the correct reading. Separately, 150.10.40.40(2) is presented as a free pass; the suite it permits must still satisfy 5.10.1.30(3) (*"A dwelling unit may not be entirely below … established grade"*, quoted in P5.6), and no `suite_interior_sqm` disclosure says so — `basement_unit_grade` (P5.12) is scoped to the RB `basement_*` fields.

**P-6 — M-204: two vocabularies for one outcome.** RT `unregulated` vs RM `not_applicable` (M-204(2)/(3); P5.2 item 1; P5.10 `main_depth_cap_m`). The distinction is defensible, but it is not explained anywhere and no sibling row uses both for the same "rule names a house" situation.

**P-7 — M-204: a by-law drafting slip not flagged.** 10.40.40.30(2) verbatim: *"a detached houseplex or semi-detached houseplex may have a permitted maximum building **length** of 19.0 metres"* — under the "Building Depth" article. A keyer following the verbatim would key a length. 10.60.40.20(1) also verbatim opens *"If a lot abuts a major street"*, so "RT has no depth or length rule except on a major street" is right but is only true for length; RT depth (10.60.40.30) could not be located in this pass (**UNVERIFIED**).

**P-8 — M-201: "Other zones are unchanged" is not observable.** 10.10.40.10(1)(B) gives 10.0 m to *any* building on an R lot with no HT; (1)(C) gives a houseplex max(HT, 10.0). On an unlabelled R lot the two units give the same number, but no row or field says which unit wins, and `main_height_m` cites only 10.80.40.10 units (which apply to RM). M-201's own conclusion may hold for R; its last sentence does not say so.

**P-9 — M-202: INV-11 still carries the old formula.** Spec 78:1385 verbatim: ``INV-11 | `dwelling_units ≤ min(u, 4 [S always], 6 iff gis_in and detached)` `` — the pre-M-202 `min`. The corrected formula in M-202 and P5.4 (`min(u [lifted to 6 only in R/RT/RM inside 600.60], 4 for S always, …)`) is a **different** expression, and the machine reads the invariant. One concept, two forms, the wrong one enforced.

**P-10 — M-203: RD 10.20.40.10(8) is a storey rule, not a GFA rule.** Verbatim: *"this space is not a storey if it is used for housing or maintaining mechanical equipment … and the floor area does not exceed 20 square metres"*. M-203 (69a:309), P5.2 item 6 and `attic_outside_gfa_sqm` (line 1097) say "a clause excludes it" **from GFA**, and the P5.12 template `attic_outside_gfa` ends *"or a clause excludes it"*. The template's ambiguity is what hides the error. The row should say "a clause excludes it **from the storey count**".

**P-11 — M-206: applied or disclosed.** "Applied in `renovation_addition`" (69a:312), "**One-storey extension (M-206; renovations only …):** … Applied, disclosed `one_storey_extension`" (P5.6), and "one-storey +2.0 m extension on RA only" (`main_length_cap_m`) all say applied; `one_storey_extension`'s template reads as a disclosure; and the row's own admission says new-build is "conservative by at most one storey × 2.0 m × half the building width". One code, two meanings.

**P-12 — M-205 scope: three bullets are unmodelled.** M-205(2) adds "RD/RS by required frontage; RT 0.9 m; RM 1.2 / 1.5 m" and RD 10.20.40.70(5)/(6), and **P5.2 item 1, `main_side_setbacks_m`, INV-9 and the P5.13 vector list none of them** (P5.2 item 1 carries only the R 0.45 m, RD (5) and the party wall). They ride the generic l2:`side_setback_m`, so the "explicit by-law-derived field" M-205 promises is generic for four of five zones.

**P-13 — M-207: the status vocabulary fights the words, and the class scope is wrong.** 10.5.30.20(2) *"a residential building may not be erected"* (prohibition) → `not_buildable`; 10.5.30.21(3) *"a detached house or detached houseplex may be constructed on that lot, and only if: (A) the lot frontage is at least 6.0 metres"* (permitted-use gate for two classes only) → `not_permitted` applied to all new-build rows, which on a lawfully-existing sub-frontage vacant lot would mark `house_with_secondary_suite` and `semi_detached_houseplex` `not_permitted:10.5.30.21(3)` although the clause names neither. P5.2 item 1 defines `not_buildable` as "a dimension under `min_dimension_m`", which is a third meaning.

**P-14 — M-207: an undisclosed lot fact.** "The lane exception is taken as not holding, disclosed" (P5.1 gate 2) has no code of its own; the only candidate is `irregular_lot_approximation` ("this lot is close to a threshold"). A missing lane width is not a threshold. The row also says the lane exception "is shown on the `not_buildable` row as its binding clause (**Spec 78 §6.5 rule 8**)" — §6.5 (Spec 78:521) is the parking table and has no numbered rules; the binding set is P5.2 item 10 and carries no binding-clause field.

**P-15 — M-207/M-212/M-213: one field, three statuses.** The same lot facts are `not_permitted:holding_zone` (new build), `gate_flags` (`heritage_designated`, `ravine_protection`, `shoreline_hazard`, `triangular_lot`, `lot_viability_margin`), P5.12 codes of the same names, and `not_evaluated:exception_not_authored` vs `exception_not_authored`. Also `lot_viability_margin` (a `gate_flags` value) and `irregular_lot_approximation` (a code) are the same fact with two names (P5.10 `gate_flags`; P5.12).

**P-16 — M-208: the operator verdict boxes cover the strictest condition only.** 150.8.50.10(1)(B) is the **85 %** branch (> 6.0 m frontage). A page verdict "disclose" on (B) means the most restrictive branch is disclosed, so a laneway suite sized against a known required landscaping area can be shown `permitted` while that area is impossible — because the area between the house and the suite is fixed by the separation (150.8.60.30), not by the suite's depth. The row asserts "it never sizes the suite". For 150.8 that is a scope decision worth stating as one (a LIMIT disclosed, not applied), not a fact.

**P-17 — M-208: the garden-suite breach claim is wrong.** 150.7.50.10(1)(A)/(B) measure *"the area: … between all rear main walls … and the rear lot line"*. A deeper suite in a shallow yard does **not** change that area; only the suite's width (across the lot) can reduce it. "The suite's footprint sits in that area, so a large suite in a shallow yard may breach it" is **WRONG** for depth; it is right only for width. The row's own conclusion (disclose, never bind) survives; the justification does not.

**P-18 — M-209: the field the test is named after does not exist.** The row names `lane_abutting_length_m` "per edge"; P5.10 persists `lane_rear_length_m` / `lane_side_length_m` (via `suite_lane_arm`), and `lane_abutting_length_m` has **no lineage row**. P5.3 and P5.5 also say the derivation is "OWED: Spec 130 / enrich_centreline WF3, KFM-78-9", i.e. the input the test needs is not built — and the row's status is RATIFIED. KFM-78-9 (Spec 78) still names a 5 m tolerance while `lane_buffer_m` defaults to 4.0 (P5.9), so two numbers govern one buffer (the P5.9 row says it "replaces the 5 m placeholder"; KFM-78-9 is unchanged). Also the "each edge classed rear / side" of M-209 vs P5.5's edge classing "rear / side …; front by `primary_frontage_street_name`" — two splits.

**P-19 — M-210/M-211/M-212/M-213/M-214/M-215/M-216: cited-but-unverified.** The 10.5.40.40 reduction list in M-210 matches what was read; the suite-GFA-article numbers, 10.5.40.71(3)/(4), 10.5.40.21(2), 10.5.40.31(2), 150.8.60.21, 10.20.40.70(5)/(6), 10.10.40.70(4)'s chapeau, 5.10.40.70(5), 5.10.40.40(1), 150.7.80.1 / 150.8.80.1 and 150.8.30.20(1) were not read in this pass — UNVERIFIED, not "correct". For M-209 this matters more than for the others: 150.8.60.20(2)/(3) **were** read and quote 10.5.60.20(2)/(3),(5),(6) and *"regulations 10.5.60.20(2) and (5) and regulation 10.10.60.20(1)"* — not the 10.5.60.20(4)/(2),(3),(5),(6) displacement set P5.5 attributes to 150.7.60.20 / 150.8.60.20, so P5.5's lane-centreline argument ("150.7.60.20 and 150.8.60.20 displace 10.5.60.20(2),(3),(5),(6) only, so (4) still binds") is stated against text that does not say it.

**P-20 — M-211: the up-room composition is two things.** "(1) … the up-storey plate still meets the depth / length caps … and, where FSI applies, `fsi × lot − existing GFA`" mixes a cap on a *plate* (depth/length, a dimension) with a cap on a *volume* (the FSI residual). P5.6 says the same. A reader cannot tell which binds when both do. One sentence fixes it; today the formula has no stated operator.

**P-21 — M-212: scope.** The triangular-lot detector (exterior ring simplified at `triangle_simplify_tol_m`, ≈ 2,027 parcels) is a new geometric data source on a parcel population. It is disclosed and small, but it is strictly more than "a disclosure over existing data"; the row should say so the way M-214 does ("no scope added").

**P-22 — M-213/M-214: the gate/disclosure pair is asymmetric.** `holding_zone` is `not_permitted:holding_zone` on new build and a `holding_zone` disclosure on renovation rows (P5.1 gate 1) — but `gate_flags` lists `holding_zone` for both, and INV-21 says *"every `gate_flags` value has its disclosure code"*: on a new-build row the value is present with a status, not a code. Whether `gate_flags` may carry a value whose row has no disclosure is not stated.

**P-23 — M-215: the entrance clause has two artefacts for one limit.** `entrance_height_m` is a Layer 2 target (P5.3; P5.10 `first_floor_elevation_m` inputs) and `main_entrance_height` is a P5.12 code the `first_floor_elevation_m` row cites as its own (line 1063). The row names only the disclosure. One clause, a target and a disclosure, with no statement of which governs when the target is `not_evaluated`.

**P-24 — M-204: the `gain_reasons` guarantee is not checked.** P5.2 item 11 promises `gain_reasons` "names each released limit that actually bound the detached row"; the field is checked by INV-6 only, which verbatim is *"`gain_reasons = none` ⇒ `gain_vs_detached_gfa = 0`"*. INV-5 checks the GFA ordering, not the reasons. The two values M-204 adds are therefore unobservable in the sense this lens requires: nothing fails if a row carries `depth_length_19m` when the length cap never bound. Also unstated: whether the S row carries `gain_reasons` at all (on a semi lot the `detached_house` row needs severance, so the gain is NULL and the reasons are moot).

## Cross-row observations (one statement, not per row)

- **Standardized, mostly.** Field names are consistent across Spec 78 / 131 / 69a except P-4, P-10, P-18, P-23. Status forms are consistent except P-13 and P-15.
- **Scope is clean.** No row in M-199..M-216 adds a scenario beyond P5.1's closed set; the only new data sources are the triangular detector (P-21) and the lane-edge classification (P-18), and the lane one is stated as OWED.
