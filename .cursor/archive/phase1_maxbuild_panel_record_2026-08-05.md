* **Root cause:** `enrich-parcels.js:504-509` computes `width_raw = GREATEST(0, frontage_m − side_count × side_setback − ravine_red)` and `length_raw = GREATEST(0, depth_m − front_setback − rear_setback − ravine_red)`, then `:514-516` does `NULLIF(width_raw, 0)` and `box_area = CASE WHEN width_raw > 0 AND length_raw > 0 THEN width_raw × length_raw END`. **Both guards clamp only at EXACTLY zero.** When setbacks consume all but a few centimetres of a dimension, the residual is a small positive number and is treated as a legitimate buildable dimension. There is no minimum-viable-dimension floor.
* **Hypotheses tested and REFUTED (do not re-litigate):**
  * *"It's the rear-yard suite being added to a main-only envelope"* — **refuted: main GFA alone exceeds the envelope in 424 of 424.**
  * *"It's degenerate/irregular lot geometry (bbox artifact)"* — **refuted: `frontage_m × depth_m > lot_size_sqm × 3` in 0 of 424.** Average affected lot is 415 m² with an 8.26 m build width; only 37 of 424 are narrow. This is not a sliver-parcel story.
  * *"It's corner/ravine parcels"* — 39 corner, 9 ravine of 424. Contributing, not causal.
* **Why nobody saw it:** the invariant `opt_aor_gfa_gt_max_buildable_gfa` is `sev: 'MED'` and **ungated** (`parcel-sanity-audit.js:128`), and **no BOUND check exists on `max_build_width_m` or `max_build_length_m` individually** — only the derived footprint and GFA are checked, which is exactly how a 0.02 m building length passed. Compounding: the chain has not completed since 2026-07-08, so no assert step has run at all in a month.
* **THE FIX SHAPE IS CONSTRAINED BY THIS — suppressing the envelope is WRONG.** Of the 28,006 residential parcels with a sub-3 m build dimension, **19,268 (69%) have a real building today, averaging a 140 m² roof footprint.** Telling a parcel with a visible 140 m² house that nothing is buildable is a different wrong answer, not a fix. And suppression would cut residential max-build coverage **97.0% → 90.6%** against an `assert_global_coverage` floor of WARN<88 / FAIL<75 — 2.6 points of margin. (A 1.5 m floor: 6,303 parcels, 95.6% coverage.)

## Findings → Fixes
* **F1 — the box term binds when it is not a valid constraint.** When `width_raw` or `length_raw` falls below a minimum viable dimension, the setback box is not a geometry we believe — it is evidence that the zone-default setbacks do not describe this lot. **Fix: exclude `box_area` from the footprint `LEAST` in that case and let the buffer/coverage-cap terms bind**, rather than emitting a collapsed envelope or emitting nothing. Record the choice in `max_build_footprint_basis` (an existing basis field) so it is observable, and downgrade `max_build_confidence` for those rows. Threshold as a `logic_variable` (`max_build_min_dimension_m`), not a literal.
* **F2 — the invariant is MED/ungated.** Promote `opt_aor_gfa_gt_max_buildable_gfa` to `gate: true` so a recurrence FAILS the chain instead of printing. **Sequencing matters: promote only AFTER F1 lands and the count is 0, or the gate reddens the chain on day one.**
* **F3 — no bound on the raw dimensions.** Add BOUND checks on `max_build_width_m` and `max_build_length_m` (`bad: < max_build_min_dimension_m` where the envelope emitted), so the next collapse is caught at its source rather than three derivations downstream.

## Known risks
* **The fallback choice is the whole fix.** Letting coverage/buffer bind is a judgment: for the 69% with a real building it is clearly right; for the remaining 31% with no building we are asserting buildability from the coverage cap alone. The panel should stress whether `max_build_confidence` downgrade is sufficient disclosure.
* **The threshold value is a live trade-off:** 3.0 m zeroes 28,006 parcels if we suppressed (we don't), and is the boundary at which the *box* stops being trusted. Too low and collapsed boxes still bind; too high and we discard valid narrow-lot geometry (Toronto has genuine 3.5 m-wide houses).
* **F2 sequencing:** promoting the gate before the data is clean turns the chain red immediately.
* **319 stored cost estimates change.** These are money numbers that have been wrong; correcting them is right, but it is a visible movement and should be called out, not slipped in.
* The chain has not completed since 2026-07-08, so step 11's re-run is also the first exercise of the assert steps in a month.
