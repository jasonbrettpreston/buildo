# Independent review — Spec 78 §Phase-5 (max-build scenarios) vs. the verbatim by-law

Scope: `docs/specs/01-pipeline/78_optimal_lot_configuration.md` §Phase-5 (P5.0–P5.13), cross-checked against `docs/reference/bylaw-provisions.json` (verbatim text of By-law 569-2013), `69a_mcbylaw_register.md` M-180..M-198 and `131_lot_configuration_policy.md`.

Method: every clause the design cites, plus every clause a correct max-build answer needs, was grepped in `bylaw-provisions.json` and the deciding words quoted. Where I could not find text I say **UNVERIFIED** rather than guess.

Legend: **CORRECT** = the design's reading matches the quoted words; **PARTIAL** = the number/clause exists but the design drops a condition it is subject to; **WRONG** = the quoted words contradict the design; **UNVERIFIED** = clause text not found in the reference file.

Zones: R = 10.10 · RD = 10.20 · RS = 10.40 · RT = 10.60 · RM = 10.80.

## (b) Missing fields / rules a correct max-build answer needs

Each row: clause → quote → does the design cover it anywhere?

### b1. Main-building setbacks

- **Front yard, 10.5.40.70(1) averaging** — *"(A) beside one lot in the Residential Zone category, and that abutting lot has a building fronting on the same street and that building is, in whole or in part, 15.0 metres or less from the subject lot, the required minimum front yard setback is the front yard setback of that building on the abutting lot; and (B) … the average of the front yard setbacks"*; zone default RD 10.20.40.70(1) *"If regulation 10.5.40.70(1) does not apply, the required minimum front yard setback in the RD zone is 6.0 metres"*.
  **Not covered.** The design takes `front_setback_m` straight from Layer 2 (P5.3). For *max build* averaging is a **smaller** setback when the neighbours sit closer to the street, so ignoring it under-builds. Nothing in P5.2, P5.10 or `disclosures` mentions averaging. There is no field that records the neighbours' setbacks was read (nor `parcel_abutments.front_setback_est_m`, which P5.5 already lists as "advisory (Q-B1 open)" — used only for suites).
- **Side yard, R blank-wall reduction 0.45 m** — 10.10.40.70(4): *"The required minimum side yard setback required in regulation 10.10.40.70(3)(A) and (B), may be reduced to 0.45 metres if there are no windows or doors in that side of the building, for the following residential building types in the R zone: (A) a detached house; … (D) a detached houseplex; … (E) a semi-detached houseplex"*.
  **Not covered — WRONG omission.** This is the same design choice P5.5 already rules on for suites (blank wall = smallest lawful setback, M-197). The R row would build 0.9 m of side yard it does not need on every interior side, on the **headline detached-house row itself**. It is not in P5.2, not in `cited_clauses`, not a disclosure.
- **RD side-yard shift** — 10.20.40.70(4): *"the required minimum side yard setback on one side … may be reduced by a maximum of 0.3 metres if the required minimum side yard setback on the other side … is increased by the same amount"*.
  **Not covered.** Also a build-more option (asymmetric shift can beat the symmetric box). Disclosed at best.
- **Lane centreline** — 10.5.40.70(2): *"A building or structure in the Residential Zone category may be no closer than 2.5 metres from the original centreline of a lane."*
  **Not covered for the main building.** P5.5 applies lane setbacks to the suite only. A `depth_search` that pushes a main building toward a rear lane can breach the 2.5 m lane line and no field or binding constraint reports it (`binding_constraint` has no lane value).

### b2. Building depth / length

- 10.20.40.30(1): *"In the RD zone with a required minimum lot frontage of 18.0 metres or less, the rear main wall of a detached house or detached houseplex, not including a one storey extension that complies with regulation 10.20.40.20(2), may be no more than 19.0 metres from the required front yard setback."*
- 10.20.40.30(2): *"a detached houseplex may have a permitted maximum building depth of 19.0 metres if the lot: (A) has a lot depth of 36.0 metres or greater and a lot frontage of less than 10.0 metres; or (B) has a lot depth of 40.0 metres or greater and a lot frontage of 10.0 metres or greater."*
  **Partly covered.** P5.3 carries `building_depth_m` / `building_length_m` and P5.2 item 1 applies them as a cap, and `depth` / `length` are `binding_constraint` values — good. But the design never states the **one-storey rear extension exclusion** (the "not including a one storey extension that complies with 10.20.40.20(2)" carve-out) that P5.6's "back room" depends on, and no field carries "depth measured from the *required front yard* setback" (which differs from measured-from-lot-line whenever front-yard averaging applies).

### b3. Lot area / frontage minimums

- `x.30.10` / `x.30.20` per building type (semi houseplex 50 %, M-181).
  **Partially covered** — P5.3 lists neither `lot_area_min` nor `lot_frontage_min` as inputs, and P5.1 uses them only indirectly through "the zone permits it". There is no field recording "the lot is below the minimum lot area for a detached houseplex" (in which case a *new* houseplex on an undersized lot is not as-of-right — RD 10.20.30.10/10.20.30.20 were not read here → UNVERIFIED).

### b5. Landscaping / soft landscaping

- 10.5.50.10(1): *"on a lot with a detached house, semi-detached house, detached houseplex, semi-detached houseplex or townhouse … (A) for lots with a lot frontage less than 6.0 metres … the front yard, excluding a permitted driveway or permitted parking pad must be landscaping; (B) for lots with a lot frontage of 6.0 metres to less than 15.0 metres … a minimum of 50 percent of the front yard must be landscaping; (C) … 15.0 metres or greater, a minimum of 60 percent …; and (D) a minimum of 75 percent of the front yard landscaping required in (A), (B), and (C) above, must be soft landscaping, and if a lot does not have a permitted driveway in the front yard, a minimum of 75 percent of the front yard must be soft landscaping."*

- 10.5.50.10(3): *"a lot with a residential building, other than an apartment building, must have: (A) a minimum of 50% of the rear yard for soft landscaping, if the lot frontage is greater than 6.0 metres"*.
  **Not covered — WRONG omission, and it binds.**
  1. None of these numbers appear in P5.10. They are not merely report fields.
  2. The rear-yard soft-landscaping 50 % (3)(A) competes directly with the **garden/laneway suite footprint** in the rear yard and with the main building's rear extension. A suite that covers most of the rear yard can make the rear yard non-compliant, and P5.5 never tests it; `suite_binding` has no landscaping value.
  3. Design choice (M-176 style, "no driveway") would set the front-yard soft-landscaping share to 75 % of the whole front yard — an unstated constraint on the front setback/parking layout.
  M-177(5) names *"the landscaping shares (150.7.50.10(1), 150.8.50.10(1))"* as **suite report fields**, but the **main-building** 10.5.50.10 landscaping is still missing.

## (c) Missing scenarios

P5.1 declares a **closed set** of seven rows: `detached_house`, `detached_houseplex`, `semi_detached_houseplex`, `house_with_secondary_suite`, `renovation_addition`, `renovation_interior`, `renovation_basement`, plus a suite add-on. It also says *"First release: as-of-right only. The CoA tier, **the townhouse forms** and the K axis (§6.2) join later."* Against the by-law, these owner-expectations fall outside the set:

1. **Semi-detached house** (as a house, not a houseplex). `800.50(746)` defines the *houseplex* family; a plain semi-detached house is the same building with one unit per side and is the dominant form in RD/RS (O-4 measures 82 K semi parcels). The by-law permits it — 10.10.40.70(3)(A)(ii) *"a semi-detached house"*; 10.20.40.1(2)(A) *"a maximum of one residential building is permitted on a lot"* with the semi as one building on two lots. **Missing.** O-4 does define `semi_half`, but only as a precondition for `semi_detached_houseplex` and the renovation class; there is no `semi_detached_house` row, so an owner of half a semi cannot see the max build of their own house.

2. **Townhouse.** Explicitly deferred ("join later"). By-law basis is ample: 10.20.40.1(4) *"the required minimum width of a dwelling unit in a townhouse is: (A) 5.0 metres if the dwelling unit does not have an individual private driveway"*; 10.20.30.20 townhouse frontage `(… − 1.0 m) × dwelling_units` (M-187 Q13); RD 10.20.40.10(1)(D)(i) *"for a townhouse, the greater of 13.0 metres …"*; 10.20.40.10(2)(C) *"the permitted maximum height of main walls do not apply to townhouses … on a lot abutting a major street"*. **Missing and material** — the townhouse is the form with the largest main-street bonus (13 m vs 10 m).

3. **Duplex / triplex / fourplex as distinct rows.** `800.50(181)(E)`: *"Detached houseplex includes the following types of building: a duplex, triplex or fourplex that complies with (A) to (D) above."* The design folds all three into one `detached_houseplex` row with `dwelling_units` as a number. That is legally right (they are one definition) but **the owner sees "houseplex", not "duplex"**. Not a by-law error — a presentation gap in a closed set that claims to be the answer set.

4. **Fiveplex / sixplex.** In a 600.60 overlay, `600.60.20(1)(A)(ii)(e)` includes *"a duplex, triplex, fourplex, fiveplex or sixplex"*, with (iii) *"Fiveplex means a building located on a single lot, that has five dwelling units"* and (iv) *"Sixplex means a building located on a single lot, that has six dwelling units"*. The design reaches 6 only via `dwelling_units` = 6 inside `detached_houseplex`. **Missing as a named row**, and there is no row at all for the case where a lawfully existing apartment building of 5–6 units is involved: `600.60.30(2)` *"A lawfully existing apartment building with five or six dwelling units … is not a fiveplex or sixplex, is not subject to the regulations of Section 600.60"* — the design would treat that lot as a houseplex with 6 units and apply the wrong (favourable) rules.

5. **Multiplex in RM.** In RM the by-law permits more than a houseplex: 10.80.40.10(1)(B)(ii) *"12.0 metres, for any other building or structure"*, 10.80.40.70 separates *"an apartment building with a height of 13.0 metres or less"* (0.9 m side yard) from *"an apartment building with a height of more than 13.0 metres"* (7.5 m). **Missing.** RM is 25,847 of the 94,532 semi parcels per O-4; an RM owner's true max build often exceeds a 4-unit houseplex.

6. **Laneway and garden suite as separate rows.** The design makes `suite_type` a **column** on every row, not a scenario. The by-law treats them as different buildings with different rules (150.7 vs 150.8); 150.8.20.1(2) *"A maximum of one ancillary building containing either a laneway suite or a garden suite is permitted on a lot"* confirms both exist as alternatives. Presenting them as one column with an "the larger objective is kept" rule means **only the better of the two is ever shown** — the owner of a lot where the garden suite wins never sees that a laneway suite is also lawful.

7. **Demolish-and-rebuild vs addition.** Only `renovation_addition` (attachment) exists. A full **new build on a lot with an existing house** is the `detached_house` row, which is right, but there is no row that says "demolish and rebuild" and no field distinguishing "the existing house stays" from "site is cleared". `10.5.40.71(1)` relief (*"if the lawful building setback of a lawfully existing building or structure is less than the required minimum building setback … that lawful building setback is the minimum"*) applies only while the building is kept — the design applies 10.5.40.71(3) but never states that the new-build rows do **not** inherit any existing relief (10.5.40.71(2): *"Any addition or extension … must comply … or be authorized by a Section 45 Planning Act minor variance"*).

8. **R garden suite: no row shows that a garden/laneway suite is *permitted* at all in R.** 150.7.60.20(2) is introduced *"Despite regulations 10.5.60.20(2) and (5), and regulation 10.10.60.20(1)"* — i.e. the R zone's own ancillary setback is displaced, and 150.7.60.70(1) is introduced *"Despite Regulations … 10.10.60.70(1) …"*. The by-law therefore anticipates suites in R, and the design's suite add-on applies "to every row", which is right, but with R's 5 % ancillary cap (10.10.60.70(1)) and no whole-lot coverage cap the R case is materially different and has no dedicated row or disclosure.

9. **`house_with_secondary_suite` for a semi-detached house, a townhouse, or an existing converted house.** 800.50(181)(F) covers *"A detached house, semi-detached house or townhouse that has one or more secondary suites"*, so the class is bigger than `detached_house` + suite. The design ties row 2 only to the detached class (P5.1 "Layer 2 class: `detached_house` + `secondary_suite`"). A semi owner with a basement sees nothing.

## Top problems (most serious first)

1. **The R-zone FSI default of 0.6 is missing entirely.** 10.10.40.40(1)(B): *"if the zone label does not include a "d" value on the Zoning By-law Map, the permitted maximum floor space index is 0.6."* The other four zones say FSI *"is not limited"*, so R is the sole zone where an unlabelled detached house is capped — exactly the case M-176(2) measured as dominant (*"432,098 of 449,674 parcels with a max build have no FSI cap"*). The whole design is written as if FSI only ever applies through a label. Every unlabelled-R headline number is unfalsifiable against this clause.

2. **RD and RM exempt only the *detached* houseplex from the storey cap and (RD) from FSI; the design treats the semi houseplex as exempt everywhere.** RD 10.20.40.10(3)(C): *"does not apply to a detached houseplex"*; RM 10.80.40.10(3)(C): same; RD 10.20.40.40(1)(C): *"does not apply to a detached houseplex, or a residential building with a secondary suite"* — no semi. R/RS/RT name *"detached houseplex or semi-detached houseplex"*. The `semi_detached_houseplex` row, its `gain_reasons` `storeys_not_applicable`/`fsi_not_applicable`, and `gain_vs_detached_*` are all wrong on RD/RM lots.

3. **Garden- and laneway-suite setbacks are set to the wrong arm in the P5.10 table.** 150.7.60.20(5): *"(A) if the side lot line **does not** abut a street, and there are openings such as doors or windows in the side main wall …, the greater of 1.5 metres or … 10 percent of the lot frontage, to a maximum of 3.0 metres; and (B) in all other cases, the greater of 0.6 metres …"*. The `suite_side_setbacks_m` cell computes "garden 150.7.60.20(5)(B) default, corner (6) larger-of" and never evaluates (5)(A) — the arm that applies on **every interior lot with a window or door**. Meanwhile 150.8.60.20(3)(C) (*"beginning from a height of 4.0 metres, an ancillary building must be set back 1.5 metres"*) is demoted to a "disclosure" although (M-198(4)) it is mandatory. Both make the suite wider than the law allows.

4. **The front-yard averaging rule is not modelled, and neither is the R blank-wall 0.45 m side-yard reduction.** 10.5.40.70(1)(A): *"the required minimum front yard setback is the front yard setback of that building on the abutting lot"*; 10.10.40.70(4): *"may be reduced to 0.45 metres if there are no windows or doors in that side of the building"*. Both are build-more directions the design has already ruled for suites (M-197 "default is the smallest setback") but does not apply to the **headline detached-house row**. Nothing in P5.10 records that either was considered.

5. **Landscaping (10.5.50.10) is absent, and it binds the suite.** *"a minimum of 50% of the rear yard for soft landscaping, if the lot frontage is greater than 6.0 metres"* (10.5.50.10(3)(A)); *"a minimum of 50 percent of the front yard must be landscaping"* with *"75 percent of the front yard … soft landscaping"* if no driveway (1)(B)/(D); corner lots *"60 percent of the side yard abutting a street"* (2)(A). P5.5 fills the rear yard with the suite without a landscaping test, and `suite_binding` has no landscaping value; the front-yard/no-driveway 75 % is a real constraint on the build-more choices.

6. **The general ancillary floor-area cap 60.0 m² / 40.0 m² is missing.** 10.5.60.50(2): *"The total floor area of all ancillary buildings or structures on a lot … must not be greater than: (A) 60.0 square metres for a lot with a lot frontage of 12.0 metres or more; and (B) 40.0 square metres in all other cases."* Only the garden suite is exempted (150.7.60.50(3)). The laneway suite is modelled as 10.0 m × 8.0 m = 80 m² with no mention of this cap, so the laneway arm can be over-built by 20–40 m².

7. **The lane-centreline setback 2.5 m is applied to the suite only, not to the main building.** 10.5.40.70(2): *"A building or structure in the Residential Zone category may be no closer than 2.5 metres from the original centreline of a lane."* P5.5's `depth_search` pushes the main rear wall toward a lane lane with no lane constraint in `binding_constraint`.

8. **`max_bedrooms` is modelled as a cap, and the bachelor rule is dropped.** 10.20.40.1(8): *"the maximum number of bedrooms permitted within a detached houseplex is equal to: (A) four times the number of dwelling units … or (B) three times …"*; (8)(C): *"a bachelor dwelling unit has one bedroom"*. The product is a function of the unit count; making it a constraint lets `max_bedrooms` shrink a unit count that the by-law did not shrink, and dropping (C) makes the product wrong whenever a studio is allowed (which `unit_min_interior_sqm` allows).

9. **Suite GFA ignores 150.7.60.50(5).** *"may be reduced by the area within a building listed in regulations 10.5.40.40 (1), (2), and (3)(A) and (B)"* — attic, mechanical attic, basement and void area. The suite is modelled as plates only, so a garden suite over a basement is understated; and the 120 m² cap is applied to the gross plate, not the reduced GFA.

10. **The renovation relief list is incomplete at exactly the clause that matters.** 10.5.40.11(3): *"Any alteration to the roof of a lawfully existing building referred to in regulation 10.5.40.11(1) may be constructed to the maximum height in regulation 10.5.40.11(1)"*. The design discloses 10.5.40.11(4)/(5) (and M-192 lists (1)–(7)) but treats 10.5.40.11(3) nowhere — it is the one addition-facing relief that needs no extra fact. Also: `10.5.40.11(5)` was **not found** in the reference file, and neither were `10.5.20.40`, `600.60.40`, `150.7.60.70(2)`, `150.8.60.70`, `10.5.30.41`, `10.5.40.21`, `10.5.40.31`, `10.5.40.41` — every one of those citations in the design is **UNVERIFIED** against the verbatim text held in this repo.

11. **Missing scenarios.** No `semi_detached_house` row (RD/RS dominant form), no townhouse row, no five/sixplex row and no *lawfully existing 5–6-unit apartment building* case (600.60.30(2): *"is not a fiveplex or sixplex, is not subject to the regulations of Section 600.60"*), no RM multiplex row (10.80.40.10(1)(B)(ii): *"12.0 metres, for any other building or structure"*), and a garden and a laneway suite are one column rather than two rows, so the losing suite is never shown as lawful.

12. **`Hw` is stated unconditionally for RD flat roofs and RM houseplexes.** 10.20.40.10(4) applies only where the roof is flat/shallow *"for more than 50 percent of the total horizontal roof area"* and (C) excludes *"townhouses or apartment buildings on a lot abutting a major street"*; RM 10.80.40.10(6) covers only *"a detached house or semi-detached house"*, so an **RM houseplex has no form (a) at all** and `gfa_flat_sqm` should be na, which P5.10 does not allow.

13. **Dormers are over-read.** 10.10.40.10(5)(B): *"the total width of the faces of the dormers … is no greater than 40 percent of the width of the parts of the main walls that do not exceed the permitted maximum main wall height"* caps dormer **width**; P5.2 item 6 turns it into "Dormers lift … 40 % of the limited wall width" to full height, which is a different (larger) envelope.

14. **Smaller, still-wrong details.** RD 10.20.40.10(8) attic exclusion needs *"mechanical equipment … does not exceed 20 square metres"* (the design omits the condition); RM 10.80.40.10(1)(B)(ii) 12.0 m and 10.80.40.10(4) entrance-scope (*"for a detached house or semi-detached house"*) are both uncarried; 10.5.40.40(3)(A) says *"**may** be reduced"*, not "excluded"; design cites `150.7.20.1(2)` (UNVERIFIED — only 150.8.20.1(2) exists in the file) and `600.60.40(3)` (UNVERIFIED — 600.60.40 not in the file); and no field anywhere records that a **Chapter 900 exception applied** even though 10.20.40.1(6)/(7) (*"the more permissive regulation prevails"*) will decide the answer on most lots.
