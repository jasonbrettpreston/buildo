City of Toronto Zoning By-law 569-2013, as amended (Office Consolidation) The new City-wide Zoning By-law 569-2013 was enacted on May 9, 2013. It has been appealed under section 34(19) of the Planning Act. Even though it is under appeal, the City's Chief Building Official and the Committee of Adjustment will apply the new By-law to applications filed after its enactment. Please consult with your advisors to determine whether the new by-law has any impact. Amendments to By-law 569-2013 have been incorporated into this office consolidation. The original by-law and its amendments are with the City Clerk's office. (2) Group Home - Type of Building in the Residential Zone Category In the Residential Zone category, a group home may be in: (A) a building that was originally constructed as a detached house; and (B) a building that was originally constructed as a semi-detached house if: (i) the building is on a lot in the R zone; and (ii) the group home occupies the entire building. 150.15.30 Lot Requirements 150.15.30.1 General (1) [Deleted] [ By-law: 550-2014 ] (2) Correctional Use - Location A group home or a residential care home used for correctional purposes must be on a lot that: (A) is not in the RD zone; and (B) has a front lot line abutting a major street on the Policy Areas Overlay Map. 150.15.80 Parking 150.15.80.1 General (1) Group Home - Parking Space Requirements A group home must have at least two parking spaces of which: (A) a minimum of one parking space must comply with the requirements for the zone and building type; and (B) no more than one parking space may be on the driveway. &copy;City of Toronto 1998-2026
