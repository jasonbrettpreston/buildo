The new City-wide Zoning By-law 569-2013 was enacted on May 9, 2013. It has been appealed under section 34(19) of the Planning Act. Even though it is under appeal, the City's Chief Building Official and the Committee of Adjustment will apply the new By-law to applications filed after its enactment. Please consult with your advisors to determine whether the new by-law has any impact.
Amendments to By-law 569-2013 have been incorporated into this office consolidation. The original by-law and its amendments are with the City Clerk's office.		 Zoning By-law No. 569-2013, as

600.5.1.10 Interpretation

(1)Purpose of a Community Overlay District Map
A Community Overlay District Map may alter, add or remove some of the regulations affecting the use of land within an area depicted on the Community Overlay District Map. [ By-law: 1050-2013(LPAT) ]
