## Context

The reviewer is given the rules (a small rules section in each spec, or consolidated specs), not whole documents. The protocol: plan reviews, grounding, fold, build, output review, grounding, fold, done; a second output review only on critical or high faults; no review of paperwork. Go to correct the six named issues.

## Decision

Operator, verbatim, 2026-11-21: "There shold be the plan reviews - the grounding - the fold (no other reviews) - the build - the review of the output - grounding and then the fold and then we are done.  Unless the output was revealing to have critical or high faults and a second review of the output maybe required.  That's it.  No review of paperwork."
