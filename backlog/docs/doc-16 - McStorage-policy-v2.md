## 1. The policy in plain words (ten lines)
1. When anyone opens a lot from the search page, the report numbers for that lot are worked out once, behind the 35 second loading screen. That covers the main page and See details together.
2. The same happens when a user tracks a lot, or types in their own lot size, house or renovation inputs.
3. The result is saved with its stamp (Layer 2 table_version, evaluator_version, settings sha), for that user and for everyone else who opens the lot later.
4. When a saved result's stamp is not the current stamp, it is never shown. It is worked out again.
5. Nothing is saved for a lot nobody opened or tracked. There is no all-lot fill (the 54 to 66 day job is gone).
6. The saved result may be deleted at any time without harm, because a missing result is just worked out again. (Purging is a proposal, open point 1.)
7. The big per-field tables (the 309 long form) stay only for the 25 test lots, the 1,921 cohort and the proof set.
8. Proof and testing use a sample of lots, never an all-lot table.
9. Cost for the opened lot is the cost rate x escalation x adjustment x the lot's saved size, worked out each time the report opens (it is not saved, so a rate change never leaves a stale price).
10. The first open can still take up to 33.6 s as built (median 10.9 s, MEASURED 20 lots), so task 5.11 (speed up the garden-suite split) stays and the screen setting rises to 35 s. The 1 s budget applies to reading a saved result.
