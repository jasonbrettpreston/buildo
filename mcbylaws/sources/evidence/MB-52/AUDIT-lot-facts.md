Acquisition: ~/Buildo (main) and ~/Buildo-wt-usescope have identical parcels fact columns (src/lib/db/generated/schema.ts, parcels). Layer 2: ~/Buildo-wt-usescope/scripts/lib/compute/resolve-bylaws.js (SQL :54-56) + bylaw-resolution.js buildLotVector. ~/Buildo-wt-b10fix assembly.mjs loads only units/context (42 lines, no lot facts); lot facts come from golden-lot puller rowToLot, mirrored by buildLotVector. Phase 3: ~/Buildo-wt-p37 compute-maxbld-scenarios.js SQL :124-136. Counts: read-only SELECT on parcels (496,536 rows) and bylaw_resolution_lots (440,093; 399,185 ungated). Unit counts from ~/Buildo-wt-usescope/docs/reference/bylaw-layer2-input.json (6,661 units); notation units/in-scope = units carrying the token / of those with a target in layer2-scope.json (32 targets).
