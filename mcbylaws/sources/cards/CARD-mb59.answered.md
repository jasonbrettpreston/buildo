# CARD answered 2026-10-29 (operator, verbatim): "FS-Q1 - this is easy to calculate the required number 3 times the number of units. what is the issue? even though it didnt include the number"
## MB-59 A0055/26SC#1 (maximum bedrooms; the notice prints no required number)
- The model calculates the requirement itself: max_bedrooms has a law source (M-72 / 10.20.40.1(8)): 3 x dwelling units for 3 or more units (4 x units at 2 units). The row is evaluated with that limit and the applicant's figure (16 bedrooms) and scored: ANSWERED (no City figure, CHAT-198 NN-Q1) with its verdict compared with the City's listed variance. It is neither NOT MODELLED nor NO ANSWER.
- General: a by-law requirement the notice states without a number is calculated by the model whenever the keyed rule can calculate it.
Rule-text line: "Where the notice prints no required figure but the keyed rule calculates it (e.g. maximum bedrooms = 3 x units), the model calculates it and the row is scored as answered."
