# Addendum to MB03-Q2, answered 2026-10-29 (operator, verbatim): ".3M is the deifnition - this needs to be a disclosure - if this is used in the build"
- The 0.3 m figure ("attached to or within 0.3 m of a building") is a DEFINITION that decides whether the platform rule applies; it is not a limit and is not scored as one.
- Whenever the max-build (or any verdict) relies on it, i.e. a platform or structure is treated as attached to / within 0.3 m of the building and the platform setbacks therefore apply, the output carries a DISCLOSURE naming the definition and its clause.
Rule-text line: "A definition figure that decides whether a rule applies (e.g. within 0.3 m of a building) is not a limit; when the build relies on it, the result discloses the definition and its clause."
