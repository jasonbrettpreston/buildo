## MR-Q2-P Design choices in the product's maximum build
- Where a by-law treats a design choice differently (e.g. flat roof vs not flat; garage facing the street or not; side wall with or without openings), the maximum build is calculated for each option and each result is shown labelled with its option. No option is chosen silently.
- This replaces M-172(3)'s silent default for the maximum build (the interaction left OPEN in batch ab, M-365 and the M-172 note).
Rule-text line: "For each design choice the by-law treats differently (e.g. flat roof or not), the maximum build is calculated for every option and each result is shown with its option; M-172(3)'s silent default no longer applies to the maximum build."
