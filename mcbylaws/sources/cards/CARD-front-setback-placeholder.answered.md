## FS-SB2 Placeholder when neighbours exist but cannot be measured
- Current rule: FS-SB1 step (3) "6.0 m, flagged needs survey". By-law: ~/Buildo/docs/reference/bylaw-provisions.md:486 (10.10.40.70(1)) "If regulation 10.5.40.70(1) does not apply, the required minimum front yard setback in the R zone is 6.0 metres" (same clause at :576, :654, :716, :793 for RD, RS, RT, RM); the averaging clause is :323 (10.5.40.70(1)). So 6.0 m is the by-law value only where averaging does not apply; with an unmeasured neighbour it is a placeholder. Held-out: within 1 m of the City on 19 % of lots (3 of 16 real), MAE 2.61 m (4.25 m real), median -1.22 m.
- Example: A0246/17EYK, City 8.05 m. Today 6.0 m (2.05 m short). Own footprint + 1.0 = 8.71; same-street median within 200 m + 1.0 = 8.87; mean 8.79 (+0.74 m), flagged needs survey.
- Recommendation: replace step (3) with a method, flagged needs survey: placeholder = mean of (a) the subject lot's own building front setback from the footprint data + 1.0 m (M-245) and (b) the median footprint-estimate front setback + 1.0 m of the other lots on the same street within 200 m (at least 3 lots, the lot's abutters excluded); if only one of the two exists, use it; else the median + 1.0 m of same-zone lots within 500 m (at least 5); else 6.0 m. Held-out (all 264): coverage 100 %, MAE 1.29 m, median -0.30 m, within 0.5 m 30 %, within 1 m 56 % (6.0 m: 2.61, -1.22, 11 %, 19 %). On the 16 real lots: MAE 1.55 m (6.0 m: 4.25 m), within 1 m 7 of 16 (6.0 m: 3 of 16). Steps (1) and (2) of FS-SB1 are unchanged and still win.
- Alternative (not recommended): keep 6.0 m flagged needs survey. Simple, equals the by-law fallback, but 37 points fewer lots within 1 m.
- Cost: one same-street lookup of parcel_abutments estimates per lot (indexed on neighbour_id, about 37 lots at 200 m) plus the subject's own footprint figure (already held as the neighbour-side row, else the Spec 130 formula); four new logic variables: fya_placeholder_street_radius_m 200, fya_placeholder_street_min_n 3, fya_placeholder_zone_radius_m 500, fya_placeholder_zone_min_n 5 (the allowance stays fya_porch_allowance_m 1.0). Fitted values are logic variables backed by a pinned audit (re-run of the held-out test), not law, as Spec 78 6.4 (bounded, fit_basis, held-out kill test, pinned re-fit audit; ~/Buildo/docs/specs/01-pipeline/78_optimal_lot_configuration.md:544) and M-245 (allowance a logic variable, needs survey mandatory) treat such values. Small-sample caution: 16 real lots; radius choice is flat between 50 and 400 m.
- Rule-text line: "Where neighbours exist but their front setbacks cannot be measured, the placeholder is the mean of the subject lot's own building front setback plus 1.0 m and the median of the footprint estimates plus 1.0 m of other lots on the same street within the placeholder radius (one of the two if only one exists), else the same-zone median within the zone radius, else 6.0 m; always flagged needs survey, and never replaces a user figure or a neighbour estimate."
